# Video Design Brief

## Style Prompt

Build a desktop-first cinematic scrollytelling visual system for a personal site about trade tools, pet IP, and practical product building. The mood should feel like an editorial character film rather than a generic landing page: warm, playful, slightly dramatic, and sharply staged. Characters should carry emotional beats while typography delivers the narrative punch. Motion should feel deliberate and scene-driven, with clear transitions between stress, relief, confidence, and delight.

## Colors

- `#F6F5F2` - primary canvas
- `#1A1A1A` - primary ink
- `#FF8A3D` - warm action accent
- `#6C63FF` - product/tool accent
- `#FF5C7A` - emotional impact accent

## Typography

- Primary / CJK display: `Microsoft YaHei`
- Data / utility accent: `Cascadia Mono`

## Motion Rules

- Favor strong entrance choreography over constant ambient motion.
- Use scene transitions to carry exits; do not empty the frame before the transition.
- Character motion should be readable in silhouette first, detail second.
- Product scenes should feel steadier and more controlled than pain-point scenes.
- Final conversion scenes should feel lighter, warmer, and more vertically buoyant.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use default blue SaaS gradients or generic dark-tech palettes.
- Do not stack multiple card layouts like a normal landing page inside a video scene.
- Do not mix cute mascot motion with chaotic camera movement in the same beat.
- Do not let text styling become childish or toy-like; keep the typography bold and editorial.
- Do not solve every scene with particle effects, floating icons, or random motion.

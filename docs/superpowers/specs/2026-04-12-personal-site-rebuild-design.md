# Personal Site Rebuild Design

## Goal

Rebuild the personal website while keeping the same four core entry themes and preserving `pretext` usage, but shift the overall experience to a lighter, faster, more conversion-oriented homepage. The new visual direction is primarily Apple-style minimalism with a controlled Disney-fairytale tone layered on top.

## Scope

This design covers the whole site rebuild direction, with homepage structure defined first because it sets the visual and narrative system for all follow-up pages.

The four fixed entries remain:

1. Pet IP
2. Foreign Trade Assistant download
3. Pet supplies international trade
4. Mini-program development

## Non-Negotiables

- Core themes remain unchanged.
- `pretext` remains in use for key title treatment.
- Homepage must be simpler and faster to load than the prior cinematic/plaza direction.
- The four entries remain intact as business/navigation anchors.
- The relationship between Pet IP and Foreign Trade Assistant must clearly support the later pet-supplies international-trade direction.

## Product Positioning

The site should feel like a professional personal brand site, not a game scene or a generic portfolio. It needs to communicate identity, product thinking, technical execution, and future business direction quickly.

The intended reading sequence is:

1. Understand the brand/personality through the pet IP
2. Understand the practical tool side through Foreign Trade Assistant
3. Understand the future business direction through pet-supplies international trade
4. Understand execution capability through mini-program development

## Visual Direction

### Recommended Mix

- 70% Apple minimalism
- 30% Disney-fairytale tone

### What Apple Minimalism Means Here

- Large clean spacing
- Strong hierarchy
- Controlled palette
- Clear typography
- Restraint in decoration
- Fast-loading layouts with minimal visual noise

### What Disney-Fairytale Means Here

- Warm, soft, slightly storybook atmosphere
- Gentle illustration/IP-led identity
- Rounded and friendly surface language
- Small magical accents such as glow, softness, and warmth

### What To Avoid

- Heavy multi-layer cinematic hero scenes
- Large decorative background systems that slow down loading
- Dense card piles or busy portal-like entrances
- Overly childish cartoon treatment
- Over-animating sections that should read like product information

## Homepage Structure

The homepage shifts from an immersive plaza model to a lightweight alternating editorial/product layout.

### Hero

A lightweight dynamic hero is preferred.

Layout:

- Left: pet IP hero visual
- Right: short theme introduction

Hero content should be minimal:

- Brand/title
- One clear positioning sentence
- One short relationship sentence connecting IP, tool, and future trade direction
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA

Motion should be restrained:

- fade-in
- subtle float/light drift
- no heavy parallax stack

### Alternating Entry Layout

Below the hero, use alternating left-right rows to present the four fixed themes in a clearer structure.

#### Row 1

- Left: Pet IP image
- Right: Pet IP theme introduction

#### Row 2

- Left: strategic narrative introducing the practical/business bridge
- Right: Foreign Trade Assistant download image + summary + entry link

#### Row 3

- Left: Pet supplies international trade introduction
- Right: corresponding product/business visual

#### Row 4

- Left: Mini-program development introduction
- Right: corresponding capability/case visual

This preserves the four entries but removes the need for four equal-weight portal blocks competing at once.

## Information Architecture

The homepage should explain the business logic instead of only showing four separate doors.

### Narrative Chain

- Pet IP provides emotional identity and brand memorability
- Foreign Trade Assistant provides practical efficiency and operating capability
- Pet supplies international trade is the long-term commercial direction
- Mini-program development shows digital execution ability and future productization support

### Required Strategic Message

The homepage must explicitly communicate that the link between Pet IP and Foreign Trade Assistant is preparation for later pet-supplies international trade, not two unrelated projects.

## `pretext` Usage

`pretext` stays, but its use should narrow to the highest-value headings only:

- homepage main title
- major section titles where expressive layout matters

Do not apply `pretext` broadly to body copy or every small label. The visual system should feel intentional, not ornamental.

## Performance Rules

The rebuild should be materially lighter than the previous homepage direction.

- Keep the hero to one primary visual asset
- Prefer optimized static imagery over layered scene compositions
- Use lightweight reveal motion only
- Avoid expensive visual overlays and scene simulation
- Limit typography effects to key headings
- Design for fast first impression on desktop and mobile

## Secondary Pages

Secondary pages should inherit the same system, but be even cleaner than the homepage.

- Pet IP page: more story and identity
- Foreign Trade Assistant page/download area: more utility and trust
- International trade page: more direction and business logic
- Mini-program page: more capability and solution framing

They should feel connected to the homepage without reproducing a large visual spectacle.

## Copy Direction

Tone should be warm, clear, and professional.

- Less decorative fantasy language
- More precise value communication
- Keep emotional softness in headlines and visuals
- Keep body copy practical and business-legible

## Rebuild Priorities

1. Reframe homepage structure
2. Define lightweight visual system
3. Preserve and re-seat the four entries
4. Tighten messaging around the IP-tool-trade relationship
5. Rebuild secondary page styling around the new system

## Success Criteria

The rebuild is successful if:

- The homepage loads quickly and reads clearly
- The four entries remain intact but feel more coherent
- The visual tone feels premium, soft, and memorable instead of busy
- The Pet IP and Foreign Trade Assistant relationship is immediately understandable
- The site supports future expansion into pet-supplies international trade

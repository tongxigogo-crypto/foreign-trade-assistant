# Site Strategy

## Positioning
- Personal professional website.
- Goal: clear value communication and trust-building.

## Primary Goals
- Make core offer understandable in under 10 seconds.
- Improve inquiry conversion quality, not just traffic.
- Keep iteration cycle short and measurable.

## Primary Pages
- Home
- About
- Services / Projects
- Contact

## UX Direction
- Minimal, readable, professional.
- Conversion path should be explicit and low-friction.
